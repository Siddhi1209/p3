DEPD SRH1 Job – Long Running Issue Update:
The long-running issue of the DEPD SRH1 job is currently under analysis by the Deposits team.
As discussed with Pushkar from the Deposits team yesterday, the issue is still being investigated, and he mentioned that providing a status update on a daily basis may not be feasible until the analysis is completed.
We also have the mail communication where it was highlighted that records with status 0, 3 and 7 are present in the SRHR table, while the corresponding records are not available in the master table as the data has been archived. Around 7 crore records are being traversed by the program, which appears to be contributing to the long-running behavior.
Our team has already written to the Deposits team requesting the expected timeline for raising the CR and promoting the fix.
No response has been received yet from the Deposits team regarding the CR timeline.
We also had a telephonic discussion with Pushkar yesterday, during which he confirmed that the analysis is still in progress.
Hence, currently the issue is with the Deposits team for further analysis and resolution, and we are awaiting their confirmation on the CR and implementation timeline
